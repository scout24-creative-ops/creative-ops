## Established Format — Reference, Not a Current Schedule

- Three optional blocks: **Learn**, **Show & Share**, **Explore**.
- Original 90-minute biweekly-Tuesday format reduced to 60 minutes from the intended 22 September edition after attention/time feedback.
- Scout Wiki in Creative Operations is the agenda/archive; Slack `#comms-team-only` is the communication surface.
- No recurring slide deck or centrally prepared contributor presentations. Dominik curates/facilitates verbally; contributors choose their own demo format.
- Wiki session template: three agenda columns, then compact notes/resources/follow-ups. Slack reminder patterns are bilingual and maintained in the [Communication Playbook](../profile/communication/communication-playbook.md).
- Explore outcomes can be stop, observe or investigate. Separate focused work can follow only with an owner and capacity.
