# AI Enablement

## Purpose

Establish a practical, recurring AI Enablement format for Marketing & Communications that helps colleagues use AI more effectively, makes existing solutions and learnings visible and creates a shared space to assess new AI opportunities without forcing every team to build its own agent or workflow.

The completed AI Team-Check is the discovery and evidence phase; the recurring AI Enablement Series is the practical response.

## Operating Model

- Bi-weekly Tuesdays, 12:30–14:00, starting 2026-09-08.
- Room: Charlottenburg (07-405).
- Dominik leads, curates and facilitates.
- Three recurring pillars: `Learn`, `Show & Share`, `Explore`.
- The Series is collaborative; colleagues contribute topics, solutions, experiments, learnings and problems.
- Actual project implementation and delivery happen outside the Series with the relevant owners and delivery capacity.
- Not every session needs all three pillars and not every colleague is expected to build an agent.
- Optional smaller follow-up sessions can be used for topics relevant only to a subset of participants.

### Learn

Purpose: build practical AI knowledge that can be used directly in daily work.

Simple framing: `Lernen, was wirklich hilft und Qualität verbessert.`

Current / initial topics include Skills, LLM Model / Reasoning, Memory & Custom Instructions, MVP Thinking and Scout Wiki.

### Show & Share

Purpose: make existing AI solutions, workflows, experiments and learnings visible so others can reuse ideas and avoid rediscovering the same things.

Simple framing: `Teilen, was bereits da ist und anderen helfen kann.`

Contributions can be finished solutions, work in progress, experiments, failures or practical learnings.

### Explore

Purpose: discuss signals, problems or opportunities and assess whether they deserve further investigation.

Simple framing: `Diskutieren, was Potenzial hat – und was nicht.`

A deeper Explore discussion can result in `Stop`, `Beobachten` or `Weiter untersuchen`. Explore should stay open when the preferred solution is not yet decided.

- Curate relevant Learn, Show & Share and Explore topics.
- Connect colleagues, reusable solutions and expertise across Marketing & Communications.
- Translate recurring questions and practical experience into useful Learn content.
- Help assess which AI opportunities deserve further investigation.
- Support selected initiatives with structure, scope, MVP thinking and strategic steering where appropriate.

## Decisions

- Treat the completed AI Team-Check as the evidence base for AI Enablement rather than as a separate active project.
- Structure the Series around `Learn`, `Show & Share` and `Explore`.
- Expect active participation and contributions from participants.
- Separate enablement from project delivery.
- Keep actual implementation outside the large recurring Series sessions.
- Use Scout Wiki as the shared session workspace and Slack for participant communication.
- Keep recurring preparation lightweight and reusable.
- Explore topics should not imply a solution is already chosen.
- Jorin supported the revised enablement direction on 2026-08-20.

## Risks and Open Questions

- How consistently teams and colleagues will contribute over time.
- How to keep contributions lightweight enough that the Series does not create presentation overhead.
- Which Explore topics justify separate follow-up and delivery capacity.
- Whether a shared Marketing & Communications knowledge source is useful enough to justify ongoing ownership and maintenance.
