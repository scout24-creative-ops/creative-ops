# AI Enablement

## Purpose

Establish a practical, recurring AI Enablement format for Marketing & Communications that helps colleagues use AI more effectively, makes existing solutions and learnings visible and creates a shared space to assess new AI opportunities without forcing every team to build its own agent or workflow.

The project combines the completed AI Team-Check as its discovery and evidence phase with the ongoing AI Enablement Series as the practical response.

## Project Phases

### Phase 1: AI Team-Check — completed discovery phase

Dominik conducted interviews across Marketing & Communications to understand how AI is currently used, what already works and which recurring barriers limit broader impact.

Core findings:

- AI is already used broadly, but with different levels of depth and routine.
- There is no fundamental acceptance problem; curiosity and willingness are generally high.
- Standard use cases are established and first specialized AI solutions are already in use.
- Larger ideas and more complex solutions increasingly expose limits around quality, reliability, time, know-how, ownership and delivery capacity.
- Existing solutions are often decentralized and not visible enough across teams.
- Not every process benefits from AI; useful selection and conscious decisions against unnecessary AI are part of maturity.

The findings are not intended to rank teams or individuals.

### Phase 2: Series concept and kickoff — completed

The Teamlead alignment took place on 2026-08-10. The kickoff concept, story and presentation were finalized on 2026-08-20, and the kickoff meeting took place the same day.

The kickoff established the operating model for the recurring Series:

1. continue as a bi-weekly 90-minute format;
2. use `Learn`, `Show & Share` and `Explore` as the three core content pillars;
3. have all participants actively contribute topics, solutions, learnings, questions and opportunities;
4. keep actual project delivery outside the Series.

### Phase 3: AI Enablement Series — operating model

The Series is built around three recurring content types. They are not a mandatory linear process and every session does not need to include all three.

#### Learn

Purpose: build practical AI knowledge that colleagues can use directly in their work.

Initial topics:

- Skills
- LLM Model / Reasoning
- Memory & Custom Instructions
- MVP Thinking

Simple framing: `Lernen, was wirklich hilft und Qualität verbessert.`

#### Show & Share

Purpose: make existing AI solutions, workflows, experiments and learnings visible so others can reuse ideas and avoid rediscovering the same things.

Initial examples:

- ScoutData
- Creative Hub
- Creative Agents
- Tone-of-Voice Agent
- successes and failures

Simple framing: `Teilen, was bereits da ist und anderen helfen kann.`

#### Explore

Purpose: discuss signals, problems or opportunities and assess whether they deserve further investigation.

Initial examples:

- AI Images — when useful and when not
- LLM Visibility
- Marketing & Communications Single Source of Truth
- Coupa

A deeper Explore discussion can result in `Stop`, `Beobachten` or `Weiter untersuchen`. Only topics worth further investigation move into a separate smaller follow-up. Actual project delivery is outside the Series.

Simple framing: `Diskutieren, was Potenzial hat – und was nicht.`

## Operating Model

- Charlottenburg (07-405) is the booked room for the recurring Series.

- Communication and contribution requests run through the existing private `#comms-team-only` channel rather than a new channel.

- The repository is Dominik's working source for topic ideas, the next-session agenda, reusable Slack post patterns and useful resources; Slack remains the communication surface for participants.

## Delivery Principle

Practical experience from initiatives such as PR Intelligence Hub, Brand Agent and Chart Agent showed that enablement and reliable delivery are different responsibilities.

For selected complex or strategically relevant solutions:

- the functional team provides the real problem, requirements, rules, knowledge, examples, testing and feedback;
- the AI lead owns and leads the solution end-to-end through prioritization, scope, solution design, technical conception, strategic steering, quality and continued development;
- appropriate delivery capacity is needed for technical implementation, integration, operation and continued development.

Dominik can contribute practically and technically where useful, but should not become the permanent builder for every AI initiative.

## Dominik's Role

- Lead and facilitate the AI Enablement Series.
- Curate relevant Learn, Show & Share and Explore topics.
- Connect colleagues, reusable solutions and expertise across Marketing & Communications.
- Translate recurring questions and practical experience into useful Learn content.
- Help assess which AI opportunities deserve further investigation.

- Keep the format lightweight and avoid unnecessary coordination overhead.

- suggesting topics or questions;
- presenting useful solutions, workflows or experiments;
- sharing successes, failures and learnings;
- bringing problems or opportunities that should be discussed or explored.
