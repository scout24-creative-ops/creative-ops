# AI Strategy & Steering

## Purpose

Provide one central strategic working and steering space for Dominik's full AI-related portfolio across the two pillars AI Enablement and AI Creative Operations.

The project supports prioritization, portfolio decisions, capacity allocation and the continued development of Dominik's role and working model. It is an overarching steering layer, not a third pillar.

## Scope

- prioritize work across both pillars;
- classify new initiatives and define an appropriate level of involvement;
- connect, delegate, pause or stop projects where useful;
- identify duplication and opportunities for reuse;
- decide which agents, builders, platforms and workflows deserve further investment;
- review where Dominik is working too operationally;
- develop the role, project structure and personal working system;
- prepare clear strategic views and decisions for Jorin and relevant stakeholders.

## Working Model

This is Dominik's central ChatGPT and planning area for questions that span multiple projects or concern the overall portfolio.

Individual project delivery remains documented in the relevant project files. AI Strategy & Steering should not become a duplicate project log. It captures only cross-project priorities, decisions, principles and strategic next steps.

## Relationship to the Two Pillars

- **AI Enablement** covers broad team enablement, orientation, discovery, shared learning and advisory support.
- **AI Creative Operations** covers Dominik's deeper responsibility for AI-supported creation, production, builders, automation and reusable infrastructure.
- **AI Strategy & Steering** guides both pillars from one shared level and is not a separate third pillar.

## Confirmed AI Operating Model

Jorin aligned with the following direction on 2026-08-20 for selected complex or strategically relevant AI solutions:

- **AI Lead:** owns and leads the solution end-to-end, including prioritization, scope, solution design, technical conception, steering, quality and continued development;
- **Functional team:** provides the problem, requirements, rules, domain knowledge, examples, testing and feedback;
- **Delivery:** provides reliable implementation capacity for technical implementation, integration, operation and continued technical development.

The model separates broad AI Enablement from reliable solution delivery. It should not assume that functional teams can build and maintain complex AI solutions alongside their normal responsibilities, and it should not make Dominik the permanent technical builder for every initiative.

## Dominik's Role

Dominik owns the strategic planning and steering of his AI-related work portfolio. He uses this space to decide where his own time and depth create the most value and how individual initiatives contribute to the wider role.

For selected high-impact initiatives, his role can extend from strategy and prioritization into end-to-end AI Lead ownership, while implementation capacity may come from other suitable colleagues, freelancers or platform teams.
