## Workflow and Responsibilities

1. **Trend Finder:** public-web discovery using taxonomy and the signal register for duplicate awareness. Lifestyle can cover celebrity/music/entertainment without a housing connection. Distinguish demonstrated attention from inferred interest; reader relevance, service value or a surprising observation may also justify a story without click evidence.
2. **Story Creator:** tests the reader question, evidence and angle, supplies full sources/limits and Do-not-claim guidance. Preserve a rejected earlier approach when a narrower evidenced story is later accepted. Internal data should answer the reader question rather than decorate it. The broader Wiki project's earlier requirement for IS24 enrichment has not been reconciled here; do not claim that reference was changed.
3. **Article Writer:** works from the supplied Story Package and owns the text. Length follows the supported story/format; short celebrity news is acceptable. Central image instructions belong in scout24-editorial-imagery and start from the final headline, intro and complete article.
4. **Human review:** factual/currentness review, accountable authorship, editorial approval and publication are distinct from technical storage or visual acceptance. Regular owners and review capacity remain a stakeholder dependency, deferred by Dominik.
5. **Portal implementation:** the intended minimal setup is the personal editorial-publisher skill plus the separately selected Contentful LPBuilder MCP (Dev). The skill defines the portal rules; MCP supplies the tools. An Agent, skill or plugin does not itself grant the connection. New drafts, rubric integration and publication are separate operations; preread exact targets and fully read back writes.
6. **Wiki maintenance:** manual/on request for this MVP. One readable case per story stores the original complete Signal, Story and Article Packages, earlier editorial versions and short actual CMS/image state. The register contains compact status/links/next-check triggers. Also add new cases visibly to the Produktionsakten overview, as explicitly requested on 9 November. Missing original packages remain missing; no reconstruction from later articles and no automatic full-flow writeback.

Use complete explicit package handoffs; do not rely on another Agent inheriting previous chat history. Preserve Writer alternatives/original image placeholders in the case and document the selected headline and actual photo separately. Prompts for another tool/chat remain complete in one code block.
