### 2026-10-26 — Scaling Editorial: package and research quality direction
- Dominik explicitly corrected the impression that no complete articles had ever been produced: **several full TF → SC → Writer drafts have already been created and critiqued**. The gap is not technical connectivity; it is compelling journalism, evidence-to-reader-promise fit, natural language and repeatable editorial quality.
- **Confirmed SC role:** autonomously challenge promising signals, verify whether angle and evidence answer a genuinely worthwhile reader question, develop additional comparison analyses / relevant internal data / primary-source external research and factual context when useful, plus targeted counterchecks. Distinguish **necessary validation** from **optional editorial enrichment** and stop when enough genuine substance exists; do not inflate output with more numbers. Can recommend story, request research or reject.
- **Package quality is still work in progress:** TF `Signal Package Standard 3.0` should pass technical observation plus limits, not fabricated causes/headline; SC `Story Package Standard 2.0` / standalone Writer Briefing should contain selected angle, question, claim, promise, evidence by source and status, claim limits, truly verified enrichment, structure and unresolved research/permissions. Writer should be able to create a meaningful article without new analysis. Distinguish existing interface standards from the **still-open job of testing completeness/reliability**.

In particular the renovation-condition piece failed because an attractive net-buyer-savings implication was unsupported by asking-price composition analysis. Do not conflate a technically successful draft with a good journalistic outcome.
- Durable quality direction: **one genuinely interesting, evidence-backed and internally reviewable article**, with stronger hook/reader relevance, credible sources, concise Writer output, low human rework; later Human Review, approved Contentful publication, and measured feedback. Data Team clarifications can run as a bounded dependency, not a reason for more open-ended matrix expansion.

### 2026-10-26 — Scaling Editorial: headline role split and three synthetic tests
- **Agreed role separation:** SC keeps editorial angle choice, central reader question, Core Story Claim, Story Promise, credibility, audience relevance and headline potential, but does **not** prescribe a preferred final headline. Writer writes/selects headlines independently, within the SC's evidence and do-not-claim boundaries; human editorial review retains final responsibility.
- Agent Builder reported updated **SC 2.0 Instructions + Story Package Standard 2.0** and **Writer Instructions**. Writer optionally accepts `HEADLINE_TEST` and generates **three different headline proposals, one recommendation and rationale**, without writing an article. **Both edits were saved to draft and explicitly said Update was still required. No user confirmation of live activation yet.**
- Three fabricated, explicitly **synthetic** tests (not Starburst claims) covered (1) purchase apartments near €300k across Munich/Berlin/Leipzig, (2) higher per-m² asking-price medians for larger flats in Cologne/Frankfurt, and (3) land listings below €200k by state. Writer selected numerically explicit "42 vs 82 m²" and "64% vs 18%" headlines in cases 1 and 3 and a more question-based comparison in case 2. Dominik was **dissatisfied with both the chosen and overall available headlines**; reassigning responsibility alone did not solve quality.
- **Working editorial definition, agreed direction (not finished reference):** Most important goal is generating **curiosity** and making the **widest genuinely relevant readership** think "this article could interest me". Express relevance in **direct, natural, simple German**, not dry data analysis, over-specific location lists, awkward phrases or template-like "what the data show" language. Build tension/reader interest from the actual story; numbers and place names are optional, only when they improve the hook or protect truthful meaning. The article must fully deliver its promise; do not invent a nationwide assertion from three-city evidence, causality, availability or a guaranteed find.
- Dominik's illustrative headline preferences: rather than enumerate three cities and their area medians, ask broadly "Wohnung kaufen mit 300k Euro? Was man dafür bekommt"; rather than jargon such as "Blick auf das Angebot" use natural direct "Wo man noch Grundstücke für unter 200k bekommt"; for rent under €1,000 avoid "wo die Suche mehr Auswahl bietet" and make the benefit plain. **These were quick directional examples, not approved final copy or an example library.** Earlier assistant-generated strong headline table was explicitly judged mediocre.
- **Next editorial job:** reduce/prioritize current long headline attribute inventory into a small shared definition and only then collaboratively curate truly good versus weak examples. Do not prematurely add a dozen generic rules to Writer or assume Fotocasa headline quality is a proven benchmark. A web research request about headline best practices was stopped by the user before completion; no findings should be treated as reviewed.
