## Updates since last summary

### AI Enablement

- 2026-08-03: Dominik conducted the final AI Team-Check interview with Caroline von Cramm, Celina Mathiske and Lena Mickel from the Social Media team. The planned interview phase is now complete.
- Social Media already uses AI daily across operational communication, production planning, translations, brainstorming, captions, community management, scripts, presentation input and visual concepts.
- The team also uses specialized tools such as Captions, CapCut and Canva AI. Some social formats, including renovation before-and-after visuals, are created largely with AI.
- Existing internal examples include structured ChatGPT projects for customer communication and production workflows, community-management responses, script preparation and an agent for checking image, music, media and usage rights.
- The team sees the biggest current value in accelerating recurring communication, planning, concept work and text preparation rather than fully automating social-media production.
- Repeated iteration is still required because scripts, dialogues and captions often sound artificial or insufficiently natural even when many approved examples are provided.
- The interview reinforced time pressure, rapid tool and model change, uncertainty about the best available tool and the feeling that AI knowledge develops too broadly rather than deeply.
- Existing agents and central solutions remain insufficiently visible across teams. The Social Media team knew of some Creative Studio solutions but did not actively use them and highlighted that many agents are built without effective cross-team sharing.
- The team needs clearer orientation on tools and models, better visibility of reusable solutions, protected time for experimentation, support with natural-language quality and clearer guidance on AI labeling, rights and compliance.
- 2026-08-09: Dominik decided to consolidate the completed AI Team-Check and the planned AI Enablement Series into one broader AI Enablement project. The Team-Check is now treated as the completed discovery and evidence phase rather than a separate active project.
- The consolidated story is: first understand where Marketing & Communications currently stands, then use the recurring findings and barriers to justify and shape the Enablement Series.
- The most important recurring Team-Check themes for the series are uneven experience levels, lack of protected time, missing orientation, limited visibility of existing solutions, difficulty reducing ideas to useful first versions, and quality or reliability problems.
- The Team-Check results landing page will remain an important deliverable, but it now serves the AI Enablement story and should lead logically from current state and needs into the series concept.
- Dominik is invited to the regular Teamlead meeting with Jorin and all Teamleads on 2026-08-10. He expects roughly 5–10 minutes for an informal alignment on the proposed AI Enablement model rather than a formal presentation.
- The planned meeting story is to briefly show the Team-Check findings, explain what those findings imply, and then present the Enablement Series as the practical response.
- The current Enablement concept document is considered too text-heavy for this discussion. The leadership version should focus on goal, overall approach, working model and the few decisions that need alignment rather than detailed agendas for each session.
- A key alignment point is how much concrete project work should happen during the shared sessions. Jorin is understood to currently see value in using the series itself for practical work.
- Dominik's current recommendation, also supported by Andreas Böhm in a recent conversation, is not to make the large shared sessions the main build environment because participant numbers and experience levels are too different.
- The preferred model is that shared sessions provide orientation, relevant AI knowledge, examples, project visibility, exchange, blocker discussion and commitment to next steps, while most building, testing and iteration happen between sessions in smaller setups.
- Protected working time remains important; the recommendation is not to move AI work back into unsupported spare-time activity.
- The current concise target statement for the series is: `Kolleg:innen sollen lernen, aus konkreten Arbeitsproblemen sinnvolle AI-Lösungen zu entwickeln und umzusetzen. Die Serie gibt dafür Wissen, Orientierung und Unterstützung.`
- The existing simple definition of an AI agent and the three building blocks `Aufgabe`, `Informationen` and `Tools` remain useful for the colleague-facing landing page, but the immediate priority is the leadership alignment and the logical bridge from Team-Check findings to the series.
