## Updates since last summary

### 2026-08-24 — Newsletter Automation discovery with Stefanie Mersmann

Dominik met with Stefanie Mersmann after Jorin suggested in the previous week's kickoff that they should connect because Stefanie was reportedly already working on automation in her area.

Stefanie works in the Content team and is responsible for the newsletters, including deciding the content, building the mails and creating the associated pages. The newsletters are primarily used to promote ImmoScout24 products, while following a newsletter-style editorial structure.

The meeting corrected the previous understanding: Stefanie is not currently working on a concrete newsletter-automation initiative that Dominik could meaningfully connect to. The bigger limitation is missing information about the recipients and where they are in their user journey.

For landlord newsletters, Stefanie sees strong performance, particularly for contextual topics such as new legislation. Tenant newsletters perform less successfully. A key reason is that the team cannot reliably determine whether a recipient is casually browsing, actively searching, has already found a property, has just moved in, or is at another stage of the rental journey. Those journey states would be valuable triggers for more relevant content, but they are currently not available with sufficient reliability.

Stefanie has also tested Central Orchestration in the B2C context. There, target groups are selected technically based on the estimated likelihood that a user will buy a relevant product rather than through manual selection. According to Stefanie, this works well only for a limited number of selected products.

Dominik and Stefanie agreed that there is currently no newsletter workflow that should be automated. Without better recipient and journey signals, automation would not solve the underlying relevance problem or enable meaningfully better content. Newsletter automation should therefore not be treated as an active automation use case for now; the prerequisite is better user and targeting information.
