## Marketing AI Landscape Check and Enablement Format

- 2026-07-21: Meeting with Jorin to discuss the findings from the Marketing AI Landscape Check and the next strategic step for AI development in the unit.
- Jorin agreed with Dominik's assessment that AI is broadly accepted but still used mainly for small individual tasks, while orientation, visibility, shared standards, reserved time and cross-team coordination are missing.
- The shared objective is to raise the unit's practical AI maturity from approximately 4 to 7 before concentrating primarily on a small number of advanced lighthouse projects.
- Jorin supported a two-stage approach: first broad practical enablement and experimentation, followed by consolidation, removal of unnecessary or duplicate agents, and selection of a few larger cross-team projects with strategic impact.
- The format should be a binding, recurring learning and working series over several weeks. Jorin will provide leadership backing, introduce the plan to the team leads and reserve protected time through a calendar series.
- Dominik will develop the concept for the format during Jorin's vacation. The concept should define the objective, initial learning phase, practical working model, handling of uncertainty, group setup, expected outputs and later consolidation.
- Jorin and Dominik intend to open the format together: Jorin provides the strategic mandate and commitment; Dominik leads the content, enablement and practical development.
- Possible elements discussed include practical demonstrations by experienced internal users, mixed cross-team working groups, work on concrete problems, and advance reflection on existing agents, desired agents and ideas for other departments.
- Exact weekday, session length, total number of sessions, speakers, group composition and final lighthouse projects remain open until the concept is aligned after Jorin's return.
- Jorin explicitly confirmed that Dominik is the right person to lead this topic across the unit through expertise, enablement and influence without operational people-management responsibility.
