## Updates since last summary

### AI Enablement Series

- 2026-08-20: Dominik finalized the concept, story and presentation for the AI Enablement kickoff in Marketing & Communications.
- The kickoff presentation uses the completed AI Team-Check as evidence for why AI Enablement is needed: AI is already broadly used, first specialized solutions exist, and larger ideas are emerging, while quality, reliability, time, know-how, orientation and visibility remain recurring limits.
- The previous operating assumption that every participating person or team should build and continuously develop its own agent or workflow is no longer the intended model. Practical experience from PR Intelligence Hub, Brand Agent and Chart Agent showed that enablement and reliable delivery need to be treated separately.
- The Series is now structured around three recurring content types: `Learn`, `Show & Share` and `Explore`.
- `Learn` focuses on practical, directly useful AI knowledge. Initial examples are Skills, LLM Model / Reasoning, Memory & Custom Instructions and MVP Thinking.
- `Show & Share` makes existing solutions, workflows and learnings visible. Initial examples include ScoutData, Creative Hub, Creative Agents, Tone-of-Voice Agent and both successes and failures.
- `Explore` is the space to discuss signals, problems and opportunities that may or may not deserve further work. Initial examples include AI Images and when they are appropriate, LLM Visibility, a Marketing & Communications Single Source of Truth and Coupa. More detailed exploration can end in `Stop`, `Beobachten` or `Weiter untersuchen`; project work itself remains outside the Series.
- The Series should not become a presentation series prepared only by Dominik. Dominik leads and curates the format, while teams are expected to contribute by suggesting topics, showing solutions or learnings and surfacing problems that benefit from broader discussion.
- Coordination and contribution requests will run through the existing private `#comms-team-only` channel rather than a new Slack channel.
- The final kickoff deck was prepared in German and an English version was requested for a colleague who does not speak German. The presentation belongs in the AI Enablement project workspace rather than the Creative Ops / Jorin Updates area.
- The immediate next organizational step after the kickoff is to define the recurring cadence, duration and dates for the next sessions. The first follow-up sessions can start with already prepared Learn topics and initial Show & Share examples before the content increasingly draws on contributions from the teams.
